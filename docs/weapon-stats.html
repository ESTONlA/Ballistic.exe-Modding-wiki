Part values and weapon totals

FieldWhere it was observed
ErgoModifierPer-part ergonomics modifier on C_WeaponPart.
TotalErgoWeapon’s current ergonomics total.
TotalInstalledErgoSeparate installed-part ergonomics total.
TotalRecoilControlModifierWeapon recoil-control aggregate.
ParentWeaponPart’s associated weapon reference.

Current and installed ergonomics are different fields. Don’t substitute one for the other in an overlay. A part may also affect recoil, weight, cooling or grip behaviour rather than just ergonomics.

    gun.TotalInstalledErgo,
    gun.TotalRecoilControlModifier

Recalculation

The Blueprint weapon base has UpdateCharacteristicsFromParts. Its nine boolean inputs in the inspected build are:

Weight, Ergo, CenterMassLoc, Recoil, GripCenter, BarrelLength,
EquippedScopeAndLaser, Cooling, TotalLength

This is a mutating recalculation function. Confirm the signature in your own dump before calling it. Prefer observing the normal attachment action first, rather than forcing a recalculation on every frame and masking a broken refresh chain.

A removal test

A local Sten fixture used a barrel strap and nested ergonomic broom grip. The grip’s modifier was approximately +0.15. Ten fit/remove cycles with the expanded compatibility hook active gave:

StepInstalled ergo
Rail-only baseline−0.600000
Grip fitted−0.450000
Grip removed−0.600000

That fixture started negative and returned to the same negative baseline. It did not show a cumulative penalty. It was a synthetic assembly, so those numbers are not the correct expected totals for every player’s Sten.

Report a stat bug

Include weapon/rail/attachment names, all three snapshots, game and loader versions, and whether the result survives leaving the workbench or restarting. Compare ordinary and expanded compatibility where possible.

Don’t clamp every negative number to zero. That can hide legitimate penalties and conceal the actual bug.

What was checked

The ten-cycle fixture ran on 6 November 2026 and returned to baseline without manual refresh between cycles. The reported real-player rail-removal penalty remains unconfirmed. The examples describe the local Windows demo setup. See Checking game updates before using them on another build.
