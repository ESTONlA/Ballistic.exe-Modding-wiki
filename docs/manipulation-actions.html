Dragging, sliding & rotating parts

Follow the grab and movement components before trying to automate bolts, levers or other movable parts.

Separate input, movement and mechanism state

The names below are recorded in the local object snapshot dated 10 October 2026. That snapshot does not identify its game build. Resolve the same names in your current build before using them; the investigation steps below are not a report that every operation was tested.

A draggable part can use a slidable or pivotable component to convert player motion into a transform. The weapon mechanism then interprets positions and transitions. Moving the mesh is not equivalent to performing the mechanical action. This distinction matters when a copied gun looks animated but never chambers, cocks or ejects correctly.

Record a normal manipulation

  1. Choose one handle or lever and record its owning actor/component path.
  2. Grab normally and observe input state, component position and mechanical flags separately.
  3. Move only partway, release, and record whether a spring or constraint restores the part.
  4. Perform a full action and record when the bolt/hammer/chamber state changes.
  5. Repeat after changing the weapon assembly to ensure the observer follows the new component.

Avoid transform-only automation

A direct SetRelativeLocation can put a bolt handle at the expected endpoint while leaving a carried cartridge or cocked state unchanged. A correct automation mod needs a validated action path, documented preconditions and interruption behaviour. Start with a visual guide that shows the player where the handle is and which stage was observed; do not jump straight to a repeated tick that forces every mechanism closed.

Release input ownership cleanly

Menus, weapon switches, loss of focus and range travel can interrupt a grab. Clear your own Lua input state and stop operating on the old component. If you changed a camera or cursor mode, restore the previous mode through a known path. A stuck grab should not be fixed by destroying arbitrary weapon actors.
