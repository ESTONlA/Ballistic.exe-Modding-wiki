Scopes, reticles & lens captures

Inspect the lens capture, reticle and aiming references as separate parts of a scope.

Identify the scope components

The names below are recorded in the local object snapshot dated 10 October 2026. That snapshot does not identify its game build. Resolve the same names in your current build before using them; the investigation steps below are not a report that every operation was tested.

The observed ScopeBase_C declares a SceneCaptureComponent2D, lens geometry, an aim direction and a reticle attachment point. A scope problem can come from any of those layers. A correctly attached mesh does not prove that the capture camera points through the barrel’s aim reference, and a magnified image does not prove the reticle is calibrated.

Reticle moves with the imageCheck whether reticle placement is a separate transform or a material effect.
Scope points away from the gunCompare AimDir, capture rotation and attachment-parent transforms.

Run a scope-only inspection

  1. Select one scope normally in the workshop, then enter a range.
  2. Log component names and transform relationships once; avoid creating a MID or render target just to inspect them.
  3. Operate zoom and turrets normally and record which plain numeric fields change.
  4. Compare the capture’s forward direction with the active sight alignment at the same instant.
  5. Switch away from the scope, then back, and check whether any transient material or capture object was replaced.

Budget captures separately

A scene capture can render another view of the world. Updating many captures or increasing render-target sizes can cost more than changing a small HUD label. Measure frame time with one scope before duplicating captures for remote players or previews. A multiplayer cosmetic model usually does not need a working private scope capture on every remote weapon.

Finish with a visual check

Parameter readback can confirm that a value was written; it cannot show that the capture, reticle and postprocess output look correct. Check the actual lens while stationary and moving, at more than one resolution and at the game’s supported aiming states. Document which layers were observed and which were changed.
