Tracing gunshot & impact sounds

Trace a sound from the loaded reference to the event that plays it before adding duplicate audio.

Separate assets, events and playback

The names below are recorded in the local object snapshot dated 10 October 2026. That snapshot does not identify its game build. Resolve the same names in your current build before using them; the investigation steps below are not a report that every operation was tested.

A SoundRef field describes a reference; a loaded Sound field describes a UObject; a playback component describes an active sound instance. Seeing one does not establish the others. Begin with the real game shot and capture its context before trying to play a second sound from your mod.

Build an audio event timeline

  1. Fire one shot and record the local cartridge and weapon identities.
  2. Observe whether the selected sound object is already loaded at the event. Do not load every possible sound inside a high-frequency callback.
  3. Compare normal and suppressed assemblies using the same distance and listening position.
  4. Record the impact sound separately from the muzzle sound.
  5. For remote effects, transmit a validated sound profile identifier and event position, not an arbitrary asset path supplied by another player.

Find double playback

SymptomPossible duplicate source
Local player hears two shotsBoth original game playback and your replicated cosmetic handler ran locally.
Guest hears two remote shotsAn event was resent without deduplication, or a proxy also triggered a real weapon event.
Reload sound loopsState packets are being treated as one-time audio events.
Sound comes from the cameraThe sound was played as 2D UI audio rather than at the remote muzzle.

Test interruption and lifetime

A sound component can outlive the visible flash that started with it. Decide whether a range transition stops outstanding audio, and reject events from the previous range epoch. Check rapid shots, duplicate network events, gun switching and a missing sound asset. Log a missing profile once and keep the rest of the range usable.
